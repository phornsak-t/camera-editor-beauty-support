Camera Editor Beauty: AI Photo

Updated October 3, 2026

About this policy

Camera Editor Beauty: AI Photo is a free photo editor developed by Phornsak Tibyakad. This policy describes how the app handles photos, editing information, permissions and advertising data. You do not need to create an account to edit photos.

Photos and on-device AI

Photos you select or capture are processed on your device for editing, collage creation and AI tools. Camera Editor Beauty: AI Photo does not upload these photos to the developer's servers or an external AI service, or use them to train an AI model. Facial landmarks, body points and image masks are used locally to apply visual effects; they are not used to identify you or create an identity profile. Edited results may be included in a draft you save.

Camera and photo permissions

The camera is used when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker or file picker lets you select images to edit. Saving to Photos requires your permission. You can change camera and photo permissions in your device's Settings; some features will then be unavailable. Camera Editor Beauty: AI Photo does not request your contacts, microphone or precise device location.

Drafts, preferences and retention

Saved drafts contain your selected images and editing settings and remain in the app's local storage until you delete them. Auto-save may save a draft when the app enters the background; you can turn it off in Settings. Theme, onboarding, advertising consent and local ad-frequency preferences may also be stored on the device. Temporary sharing files are created for export and normally removed after the share sheet closes. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local app data, but not images already saved to Photos or copies shared elsewhere.

Export, metadata and sharing

Exports can contain camera, date and other image metadata. Keep Safe removes GPS metadata, Strip All removes available source metadata, and Keep Original may retain location information already embedded in a photo. Choose Strip All when you do not want to share source metadata. When you save or share an image, Apple services or the app you choose may store or upload it according to their own settings and privacy policies. The developer does not control those copies.

Advertising and third-party data

Camera Editor Beauty: AI Photo uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. These services may transmit IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention and may share it with advertising partners. Data handling depends on SDK configuration, your region, permissions and consent choices. On-device photo processing does not mean the advertising services collect no data. See Google's Privacy Policy for its retention, international processing and data controls.

Your advertising choices

Where required, a Google consent message allows you to make privacy choices. Settings > Privacy Options lets you revisit those choices when the option is available for your region and consent setup. Your device's tracking permissions also apply; advertising identifiers are not available for cross-app tracking without the required permission. The app does not include its own tracking-permission prompt. A rewarded video is optional and is requested when you choose the ad-supported 4K export option; ordinary editing does not require watching it.

Deletion and privacy rights

You can delete individual projects using Delete Draft in Saved Drafts. Turn off Auto-save if you do not want new automatic drafts. Delete exported images separately in Photos and in any service to which you shared them. Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information handled by advertising providers; consult their privacy policies and data controls. Camera Editor Beauty: AI Photo has no developer-hosted photo account to delete.

Security and children

Local editing data is kept within the app's device storage and is subject to the device's security protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. The app is not specifically designed for children. Parents and guardians should supervise use of photo sharing and advertising services and review the applicable privacy choices.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The updated date identifies this version. Material changes requiring new permission or consent should be presented before the related processing begins.
